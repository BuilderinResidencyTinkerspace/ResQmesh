# Week 4

**Goal this week:** Receive ordered components, inspect incoming shipments, and begin hardware assembly.

## What we did

- Monitored parcel tracking and delivery statuses for the ordered flight components (XIAO ESP32-S3, MPU9250 IMU, 720 coreless motors, LiPo batteries, and MOSFET drivers).
- Little to no hands-on progress was made this week; project progress was largely stalled while waiting on pending supplier deliveries.

## Problems and blockers

- **Shipping & Transit Delays**: The ordered components faced courier delays and did not arrive during the week, creating an unavoidable bottleneck for physical assembly, soldering, and bench testing.
- **Project Stagnation**: Without the actual microcontrollers, motors, and IMU hardware on hand, practical integration work was paused.

## Decisions

- **Hold Off on Partial Assembly**: Decided to wait until all core electronics arrive rather than assembling incomplete sub-modules, ensuring cleaner single-pass wiring and soldering.
- Prepared the testing bench and soldering tools to begin immediate assembly once shipments arrive.

## Next week

- Follow up on parcel deliveries and receive the remaining hardware shipments.
- Unbox, inspect, and verify basic functionality of all components (IMU I2C communication, motor continuity, and LiPo voltages).
- Begin soldering the AO3400A MOSFET driver circuit and mounting electronics onto the frame.
